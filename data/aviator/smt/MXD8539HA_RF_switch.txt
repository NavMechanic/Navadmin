DESCRIPTION:
3P3T (Three-Pole Three-Throw) CMOS SOI RF Switch with MIPI control interface.
Operating frequency: 0.4 to 5.8 GHz.
Applications: Simultaneous voice and LTE systems, antenna switching, multi-band operation.

PACKAGE: LGA-16, 2.0mm x 2.0mm x 0.55mm, MSL1

PIN CONFIGURATION (16-pin LGA):
Pin 1: USID (RFFE Slave ID Select)
Pin 2: RF2; Pin 3: GND; Pin 4: RF4; Pin 5: GND
Pin 6: RF6; Pin 7: GND; Pin 8: RF5; Pin 9: GND
Pin 10: RF3; Pin 11: GND; Pin 12: RF1
Pin 13: NC; Pin 14: VIO (Supply); Pin 15: SDATA; Pin 16: SCLK
Ground Paddle: GND

RF SWITCH MODES (Register_0 for RF1/RF3, Register_1 for RF5):
RF1-RF2: D[2:0]=001; RF1-RF4: D[2:0]=010; RF1-RF6: D[2:0]=011
RF3-RF2: D[5:3]=001; RF3-RF4: D[5:3]=010; RF3-RF6: D[5:3]=011
RF5-RF2: D[2:0]=001; RF5-RF4: D[2:0]=010; RF5-RF6: D[2:0]=011
