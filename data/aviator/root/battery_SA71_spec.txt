PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document contains specific electrical, mechanical, environmental, packaging, and general Motorola manufacturing and quality requirements for the SA71 battery Lithium-ion polymer battery.

DESCRIPTION
Lithium-ion battery assembly with typical capacity of 7100mAh and approximately 3.72V. The battery has two B2B connectors. Includes dual-protection circuit. Battery ID: ATLSA71NVT

ELECTRICAL REQUIREMENTS
Capacity: Minimum 6890mAh, typical 7100mAh.
Standard charge (15-35C): CC 18A to 4.19V, then 12.4A to 4.23V, then 10A to 4.4V, CV to 8A, then 8A to 4.53V, CV taper to 345.5mA.
FFC charge (15-25C): Termination at 4.56V / 1134mA.
AC Impedance (1000Hz): 60mOhm max.
ESR: 190mOhm max near 0% SOC.
Designed to withstand 12.0V / 20.0A input.
Ships at ≤30% SOC (ICAO/IATA Packaging Instruction 965), voltage 3.610-3.760V.
Protection circuit draw: ≤100uA normal, ≤50uA sleep.
Zero Volt Charging: permit when cell ≥1.5V (Southchip) / ≥1.4V (Mitsumi).

Overload Current (Southchip - shall NOT open):
- 13.3A discharge for 716ms
- 16.9A discharge for 19.2ms
- 23.3A discharge for 168us
- 19.1A charge for 19.2ms

Overload Current (Southchip - shall OPEN):
- 16.8A discharge within 4680ms
- 23.2A discharge within 89.6ms
- 33A discharge within 700us
- 23A charge within 89.6ms

Short Circuit: Opens discharge path within 700 microseconds.
Under Voltage Detection: 2.30±0.040V cutoff, resets at 2.50±0.070V (Southchip/Mitsumi).
Over-Voltage Protection: Opens charge path within 1.33s at 4.650±0.020V, resets at 4.450±0.055V.

GasGauge Design:
- Chem ID: 34 38 36 37 39 37 00 00
- FW Version: 1.3.0 M0t6
- Manufacturer Name: NVT 00000004
- IC: MPC8011B (U3)
- CC Gain: 0.9-1.1

Critical 100% Inspection: OCV, discharge OC cutoff, charge OC cutoff, AC impedance, final OCV, over-voltage protection.

MECHANICAL REQUIREMENTS
Thickness measured with 2kg plate force. Width/length with 300g max force.
Critical dimensional parameters [*]: CPK≥1.33
Weight: approximately 83g (Ref.)

SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
No smoke, sparks, or flame during tests.
High current charge: 4.56V/20A for 30 minutes.
High Voltage: 12.0V/1A for 24 hours.
Rapid Charge: 4.56V/18A to cutoff 345.5mA.
Hard Short: 0.050 ohm for 24 hours.
Soft Short: 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0 ohm for 1 minute each.
Deep Discharge: 10 ohm for 96 hours.

MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS: All components lead-free and halogen-free.

PACKAGING: Per 12000362020. Ships UN 3480, ICAO/IATA, US DOT 49 CFR 173.185.

STANDARDS: CE, CB IEC62133/IEC62368, UL1642, IEEE 1725, China GB31241-2022, BIS (India), TISI (Thailand), GOST (Russia), BSMI (Taiwan), PSE (Japan), RCM (Australia), KC (South Korea).

WARRANTY: One year from receipt.
